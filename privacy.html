NOVA Player privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · applies to NOVA Player for Fire TV

NOVA Player is a media player for playlists and logins that you add yourself. We built it to keep what you do in it on your device. We do not run accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share personal data.

What NOVA Player keeps on your device

  • The sources you add: playlist and guide addresses, and for Xtream Codes logins the server address, user name and password.
  • Your favourites, recently watched channels, watch progress, reminders, Home rows and settings.
  • Your parental-control PIN, stored only as a salted hash.

This information is stored in NOVA Player's private storage on your Fire TV. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Who NOVA Player connects to

  • The servers you add. NOVA Player downloads your playlist, programme guide, artwork and streams from the addresses you enter, and sends your login to that server only, as your provider requires. Those services are run by your provider, under its own privacy policy.
  • Amazon. NOVA Player Premium is bought and managed through Amazon In-App Purchasing. Amazon handles payment; we never see your payment details. NOVA Player asks Amazon whether your subscription is active.

NOVA Player sends no information to its developer.

Children

NOVA Player is not directed at children. It has a parental-control PIN, and once a PIN is set it locks categories whose names suggest adult content.

Removing your information

Remove a source in Sources, clear history in Settings > Stored on this device, or uninstall NOVA Player to delete everything it stored. Manage or cancel NOVA Player Premium in Your Memberships & Subscriptions on Amazon.

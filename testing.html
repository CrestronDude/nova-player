NOVA Player testing instructions

For Amazon's app reviewers · NOVA Player for Fire TV, version 1.0.0

NOVA Player is a media player for the viewer's own IPTV playlists (M3U) and Xtream Codes logins. It contains no channels or content of its own, so a source has to be added to test it. Please use our sample source, which holds only freely licensed material (the Blender Foundation's open movies, Creative Commons Attribution, and public HLS test streams from Unified Streaming, Apple and Mux).

  1. Open NOVA Player. The first screen is Add your first source (the labels below are the English ones; the app follows the Fire TV's language).
  2. M3U / M3U8 link is already selected. Select Playlist address and enter https://crestrondude.github.io/nova-player/tv.m3u (the same playlist is also at https://crestrondude.github.io/nova-player/sample/playlist.m3u). Name (optional) can stay empty. Select Connect.
  3. Home shows the sample: Live TV (4 channels), the TV Guide (filled for 60 days), Movies (4 open films).
  4. Watching needs NOVA Player Premium, our yearly subscription (in-app item novaplayer.premium, term novaplayer.premium.yearly, 7-day free trial). Select any channel or film: the player shows the offer with Amazon's price and trial, Start free trial, Restore purchase and Not now. Please use your usual test account to subscribe; after the purchase the chosen channel or film plays at once. Upgrade to NOVA Player Premium (top right, beside the clock, on Home and in every section that plays) opens the same offer; it disappears once the subscription is active. Settings > NOVA Player Premium shows the subscription and offers Restore.
  5. Everything works with the Fire TV remote: D-pad, Select, Back, Menu (options for the focused item; Home rows can be moved or hidden there), Rewind/Fast-forward (a page down long lists), Play/Pause.
  6. Languages: NOVA Player follows the Fire TV's language. Settings > Display > 🌐 Language switches between all 22 (each listed in its own script) without leaving the screen; Automatic returns to the Fire TV's language.

Network

The manifest allows cleartext http because many viewers' own playlist servers use plain http. NOVA Player connects only to the addresses the viewer enters, and to Amazon for the subscription.

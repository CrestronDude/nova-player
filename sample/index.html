NOVA Player sample source

Freely licensed test content for checking NOVA Player, a player for the viewer's own playlists. NOVA Player contains no channels or content of its own.

Use it in NOVA Player

Add a source → playlist (M3U) address:
https://crestrondude.github.io/nova-player/sample/playlist.m3u

The guide is read from the playlist automatically (https://crestrondude.github.io/nova-player/sample/guide.xml).

What it contains

Four channels made from public streaming test streams (Akamai and Apple HLS test streams, and Mux test streams of the Blender Foundation's open movies) and four films, the Blender Foundation's open movies, served by the Internet Archive.
